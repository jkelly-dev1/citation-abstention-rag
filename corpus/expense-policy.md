## Approval thresholds

Any single expense of 500 USD or less is approved by the employee's direct
manager. Expenses above 500 USD and up to 5,000 USD require approval from a
department director. Expenses above 5,000 USD require written approval from the
Chief Financial Officer before the expense is incurred.

## Submission deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction date.
Reports submitted after 60 days are rejected by the system and require a
written exception request signed by a department director.

## Non-reimbursable items

The company does not reimburse traffic fines, personal entertainment,
in-flight gambling, or airline lounge memberships. Alcohol is reimbursable only
when it appears on a client entertainment receipt with the client organization
named on the report.

## Corporate card use

Corporate cards may be used for business travel, client entertainment, and
approved software subscriptions. Personal use of a corporate card is a policy
violation and must be repaid within 14 days of discovery.
