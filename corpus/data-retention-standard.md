## Retention periods

Trade confirmations and order records are retained for 7 years from the date of
the transaction. General business correspondence is retained for 3 years.
Recruitment records for candidates who were not hired are retained for 12
months and then destroyed.

## Legal hold

When the Legal department issues a legal hold, all deletion for the named
records stops immediately. A legal hold overrides every retention period in
this standard, including the 12 month recruitment period.

## Storage location

Records covered by this standard are stored on company controlled
infrastructure. Copying covered records to a personal device or to a
third party service that has not completed vendor review is prohibited.

## Destruction

Destruction of covered records is logged. The log entry records the record
identifier, the retention rule applied, the date of destruction, and the
approver. Destruction logs are themselves retained for 7 years.
