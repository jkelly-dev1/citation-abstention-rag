## Reported revenue

Acme Holdings reported revenue of 412.6 million USD for the fourth quarter of
fiscal year 2025. Revenue for the full fiscal year was 1,580.4 million USD.

## Segment detail

The Platform segment contributed 268.1 million USD of fourth quarter revenue.
The Services segment contributed 144.5 million USD.
